## #PhD_student

I'm a Ph.D. student at [Phenix research group](https://phenix.citi-lab.fr)
([CITI Lab](https://www.citi-lab.fr) - [INSA Lyon](https://www.insa-lyon.fr)).
The subject of my PhD is **Frugality of programming languages, relationship with software development practices**.

I've a strong interest in:

- the degrowth of programming languages and their tools
- computing [within limits](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Planetary_boundaries)
- [science and technology studies - STS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Science_and_technology_studies)

Studying the [Lua programming language](https://www.lua.org) is the starting point of my PhD.
(Minimalism as a main value, few core concepts enabling multi-paradigm).
